Procedure: Open reduction internal fixation of right bimalleolar ankle fracture

OPERATIVE TECHNIQUE:
The patient was positioned supine with a bump under the ipsilateral hip. A thigh tourniquet was applied. The right lower extremity was prepped and draped in sterile fashion.

A lateral approach was made over the fibula. The fracture was identified and reduced anatomically. A 6-hole one-third tubular plate was applied with cortical screws achieving excellent fixation.

A medial approach was then made over the medial malleolus. The fracture was reduced and fixed with two 4.0mm partially threaded cancellous screws with washers. Excellent reduction was confirmed under fluoroscopy.

The wounds were irrigated and closed in layers. A short leg splint was applied.

OPERATIVE TECHNIQUE:
Under general anesthesia, the patient was positioned supine. The abdomen was prepped and draped in sterile fashion. A 12mm trocar was placed at the umbilicus using Hasson technique. CO2 insufflation was initiated to 15mmHg. Additional 5mm trocars were placed at the midline suprapubically and at the left lateral border of the rectus muscle.

The peritoneum was incised lateral to the medial umbilical ligament and dissection was carried out in the preperitoneal space. The direct space, indirect space, and femoral space were completely dissected. A large indirect hernia sac was identified and completely reduced. The lateral femoral cutaneous nerve and genitofemoral nerve were identified and preserved.

A 15x10cm polypropylene mesh was placed to cover the myopectineal orifice and secured with tacks medial to the anterior superior iliac spine and lateral to the midline. The peritoneum was then re-approximated over the mesh with running suture.

Trocars were removed under direct visualization. Fascia was closed at the umbilical port. All skin incisions were closed with dermabond.
